# Finger Shadow Theatre

Developed by AHALab X ZimaBlueAI

Make a puppet, move it with your fingers, rehearse a short English scene and write about the actions you observed.

[Course home](code/index.html?lang=en) · [Local setup](code/README.md) · [Original course article](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/sBJoPbetgkh9iIuMOeFJxA) · [Licences and credits](ATTRIBUTION.md)

Course pages and teaching interfaces offer 16 languages. Choose a language on the page. English rehearsal lines and Chinese writing tasks retain their learning language, and students’ own words are not automatically translated. The writing activities are linked to grades 3–6 in Chinese school textbooks; adapt them to your class.

The authored package contains code, teaching materials and original course images. Download and configure models, third-party runtime dependencies and classroom audio separately using the setup instructions. Windows and macOS startup instructions are included; macOS hardware testing remains to be completed.
